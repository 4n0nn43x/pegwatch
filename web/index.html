Method and data

One engine, three layers, no key needed.

CoinMarketCap RWA API

GET /v5/real-world-assets/map (top 200 assets with tokens, 0 credits) and GET /v5/real-world-assets/quotes/latest?rwa_id=… (every wrapper's price, issuer, volume, plus the perp pseudo-token; 1 credit per 200 assets). Polled every 5 minutes. Feedback on what the API lacks is in FEEDBACK.md.

Real-asset reference

Ostium price feed (session prints, last close, XAU and XAG spot) and the Hyperliquid xyz oracle (moves 24/7). NYSE regime from the New York clock and the holiday calendar.

P2P dollar

Public P2P order books of Binance, Bybit and OKX for USDT in XOF, XAF, NGN, GHS, KES, EGP, both sides, three pages, payment methods normalised to rails. Official rate: EUR peg for CFA francs, interbank for the rest.

Who may buy what

A hand-checked matrix of 17 issuers × 11 countries from their terms and restricted-country lists, with redemption terms and KYC path. Elsewhere, likely means the issuer publishes an exclusion list the country is not on; unclear means it publishes nothing. We say so rather than guess.

Everything on this page is a static JSON you can read directly: world.json, ladder/<CC>.json, premiums/latest.json, fixing/latest.json. Information only, no advice, no execution.
